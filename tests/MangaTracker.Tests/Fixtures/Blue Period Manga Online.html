Blue Period

Blue Period is a powerful Japanese manga by Tsubasa Yamaguchi that tells the story of Yatora Yaguchi, a smart yet unfulfilled high school student. One day, he discovers the joy of painting and feels truly alive for the first time. Because of this new passion, he decides to chase his dream and enter the Tokyo University of the Arts. Along the way, Yatora faces fear, pressure, and self-doubt, but he also finds friendship and purpose. Moreover, the manga explores creativity, identity, and emotional growth with warmth and honesty. In the end, Blue Period shows how art can change lives and inspire courage.
